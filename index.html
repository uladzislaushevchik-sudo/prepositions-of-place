Базовые: in / on / at

in the box, in London, in the kitchen, in a car
ON на поверхности
on the table, on the wall, on the second floor, on the bus
AT точка / место
at the door, at the station, at home, at work

among — среди (3+)
in front of — перед
behind — за
opposite — напротив
inside / outside
